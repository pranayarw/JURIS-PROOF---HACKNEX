Public Legal Help

This information is general guidance and does not constitute legal advice.

How to organize evidence

  • Keep originals safe; work with copies, never markings on originals.
  • Note for each document: what it is, who gave it, when, and where it came from.
  • Create a dated chronological file of events with supporting documents.
  • Record how each item was obtained so its chain of custody can be explained.

What documents to preserve

  • Written agreements, notices, letters, emails and messages (with headers/metadata).
  • Receipts, invoices, bank statements and transaction records.
  • Identity/address proofs relevant to the parties involved.
  • Photographs, call logs and recordings, noted with date, time and source.

How to prepare before contacting a lawyer

  • Write a short timeline: dates, events, people involved, amounts.
  • Gather the documents listed above in one organized folder.
  • Note precisely what you want to achieve (refund, protection, complaint).
  • List questions you need answered; bring copies, keep originals.
